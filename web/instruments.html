SIDKid Instrument maker
Song player

An instrument is how a note sounds: a volume envelope, plus up to three small programs the player runs 50 times a second while the note plays. The wave program picks the waveform and pitch, the pulse program shapes the pulse width, and the filter program moves the filter. Instruments can share programs.
